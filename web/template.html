How it works

📤

Upload

CSV or Excel attendance, test results and timetables. Messy IDs, duplicate rows and mixed date formats are cleaned automatically.

🧮

Analyse

Attendance per subject and the recovery count ceil((0.85·T − A) / 0.15), plus weak or falling marks and a 0–100 risk score.

✉️

Alert

AI-written personal emails for students, digests for subject teachers and advisers, and an automatic voice call below 85%.

📅

Book a slot

Students pick a free period from the teacher's timetable; both get a calendar invite.

📊

Dashboard

Department and subject risk, with the most at-risk students first.

🗓️

Weekly summary

An AI-written report goes to the HOD every Monday via a scheduled job.

Built with Python, Streamlit, pandas, Plotly, Claude, Gmail SMTP, Twilio and GitHub Actions.
